# Minigame 1
## Devlog

Personally speaking, the relationship between Components, GameObjects, and Scenes seems like a cooking process. They all follow a logic from individual parts to a complete whole: one stage builds upon another until the final one emerges.


Components are like seasonings, preparations, and cooking methods, such as soy sauce, vinegar, chili powder, salt, and sugar, as well as cutting ingredients into desired shapes, marinating them, and using different methods like frying, roasting, or boiling. GameObjects are like different ingredients, each with its own characteristics requiring specific handling. For example, meat might need a longer cooking time than vegetables. Different dishes also call for different seasonings, preparations, and cooking methods. Finally, when these properly prepared ingredients are combined, they form a complete dish. Just as many different GameObjects come together to create a complete Scene.
